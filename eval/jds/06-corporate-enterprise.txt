Senior UX Designer — Aviva
London (Bishopsgate), 3 days/week in office, Permanent
£75,000–£90,000

Aviva is one of the UK's leading insurance, wealth, and retirement businesses with 19.6 million customers across the UK, Ireland, and Canada. We are a FTSE 100 company employing over 22,000 people.

Reporting Line
This role reports to the Head of UX within the Customer Digital Experience function, which sits inside the Group Customer Office. The role is part of the Wealth & Retirement design team of 14 designers across UX, UI, and research.

Purpose of the role
The Senior UX Designer will support the delivery of customer experiences across our wealth and retirement digital products, working as part of an established design team within a regulated financial services environment.

Key responsibilities
- Deliver UX design across pension, ISA, and retirement product flows
- Work in compliance with our design system (Aviva Brand 3.0)
- Collaborate with Product Owners, BAs, and Tech Leads in a SAFe-aligned delivery model
- Contribute to design reviews, journey maps, and quarterly OKR planning
- Support user research activities led by the Research practice

Skills and experience required
- 5+ years of UX design experience, ideally in financial services
- Working knowledge of WCAG 2.2 AA, FCA conduct principles, and SAFe
- Strong ability to work within a structured design system and governance model
- Experience operating within a matrixed organisation

What we offer
£75,000–£90,000 + bonus + pension (up to 14% employer contribution). 29 days holiday + bank holidays. Private medical. Share save scheme.
