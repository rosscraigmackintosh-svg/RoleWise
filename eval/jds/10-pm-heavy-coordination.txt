Senior Product Designer, Customer Platform — Monzo
London (Hybrid, 2 days/week in office), Full-time
£95,000–£115,000 + equity

About Monzo
Monzo is the UK's largest digital bank, serving over 10 million customers. We were founded in 2015 and now have offices in London, Cardiff, Dublin, and the US.

The role
You will join the Customer Platform group, which sits at the intersection of nine product squads, the data org, and Customer Operations. Your work will rarely ship as a single feature — it will mostly show up as small changes across other teams' surfaces.

Customer Platform owns the cross-cutting customer record: how we represent a customer across every surface they touch, how product teams read and write to that record, and how Customer Operations sees the same customer when they pick up the phone.

What you will work on
- Aligning nine product squads around shared customer-record patterns
- Working with three principal PMs on the platform roadmap
- Designing internal-tools surfaces for Customer Operations (300+ daily users)
- Influencing data-model and event-shape decisions alongside engineering
- Quarterly OKR-setting with the Customer Platform PM and EM trio

What we are looking for
- 7+ years product design, including significant cross-team coordination work
- High tolerance for politics and stakeholder load
- Comfort being one of many voices, not a sole owner
- Experience designing both customer-facing and internal-tool surfaces

What we offer
£95k–£115k + equity. Hybrid, 2 days/week in our London office. Healthcare, pension, share scheme.
