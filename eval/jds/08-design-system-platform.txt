Staff Product Designer, Design Systems — Atlassian
Remote (UK/EU), Full-time

About Atlassian
Atlassian was founded in 2002 and is a global leader in collaboration software. Our products — Jira, Confluence, Trello, Bitbucket, Loom — are used by over 300,000 customers worldwide including 80% of Fortune 500 companies.

The role
You will join the Atlassian Design System team and own a horizontal slice of the system used across all of our products. Specifically: tokens, primitives, and the migration tooling that lets 200+ product designers consume the system consistently.

This is not a product feature role. The customers of your work are other designers and engineers inside Atlassian. Your output is API surface, documentation, contribution patterns, and adoption tooling.

What you will work on
- Token architecture and the multi-product theming model
- The primitive component layer and its contribution model
- Migration tooling that helps product teams move off legacy patterns
- Governance — how a 200+ designer org makes shared decisions on the system
- Partnership with the design systems engineering team on Figma-to-code parity

What we are looking for
- 8+ years product design, including at least 3 years on design systems
- Strong systems thinking and API design instincts
- Experience operating as an internal platform — your customers are designers
- Comfort with multi-year horizontal work, not feature shipping

What we offer
£135k–£165k base + significant RSU package. Fully remote in UK/EU. Health, equity, learning budget.
