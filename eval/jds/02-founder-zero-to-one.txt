Founding Product Designer — Verra
Remote (Europe), Full-time

About Verra
We are a Series A startup building the agentic CRM. We closed our $12M Series A in February and are a team of nine — three founders, four engineers, one growth, and you.

The role
You will be our first dedicated designer. You will own the product surface end-to-end: brand, UI, UX, design system, and the activation surface. We have a working prototype but no consistent design language. We need someone to define what Verra feels like and ship it.

This is a zero-to-one role inside a team that is figuring out product-market fit in real time. We expect you to work directly with the CEO and CTO on weekly product decisions and to operate as a peer to the engineering leads, not as a service function.

What you will work on
- Defining the activation surface from sign-up through first agent run
- Establishing the design system from scratch — components, tokens, motion
- Working alongside engineering on the in-product agent UI
- Owning the marketing site, pricing page, and demo flow
- Shaping the product narrative alongside the founders

What we are looking for
- 7+ years shipping product design, at least one founding or first-designer role
- Strong craft — your work has shipped at a high quality bar
- Comfort with ambiguity and a fast-moving product surface
- Experience designing for AI-native or agent-first products

What we offer
Salary $140,000–$170,000 USD + 0.5–1.0% equity. Remote within EU hours.
