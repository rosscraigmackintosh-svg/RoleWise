Senior Mobile Interaction Designer
* Experis
* London (Hybrid)
* 12 month contract, Day rate: £600 Inside IR35

About the role

We are looking for a Senior Mobile Interaction Designer to join a high-profile public sector programme. The role focuses on designing intuitive, accessible mobile experiences (iOS and Android) for citizen-facing services aligned with the NHS and GOV.UK design standards.

You'll work as part of a multidisciplinary delivery team alongside service designers, user researchers, content designers, frontend developers, and product managers. The work involves designing complex service flows, validating against WCAG 2.2 AA, and producing high-fidelity prototypes that can be tested with users.

Key experience required

- Strong portfolio of mobile interaction design work across iOS and Android
- Experience designing for public sector or other regulated environments
- Experience working to government service standards (GDS, NHS Service Manual)
- Lightweight code (HTML/CSS) for prototyping is welcome but not essential
- Experience facilitating workshops and presenting work to senior stakeholders
- Comfortable working in agile, multidisciplinary teams

What you'll do

- Design and prototype mobile-first interaction flows for citizen services
- Work alongside service designers, researchers, and developers
- Validate designs against WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standards
- Align outputs with NHS and GOV.UK service standards
- Participate in user research sessions and iterate based on findings

About you

You are someone who enjoys solving complex problems through clear, accessible design. You have a strong eye for craft and are comfortable working in a structured public sector delivery environment.

Application

To apply, please send your CV and portfolio. Successful candidates will be invited to a portfolio review followed by a technical interview.
